## Sub-category shop pages — dlxprint.com attempt, reverted (2026-11-16)
Tried reusing photos from dlxprint.com (a Dubai printing company, the client's own reference for this layout) for 18 of the 26 sub-categories that still show the concept plate. **Reverted the same day**: every photo checked (business cards, booklets, printing-companies, aluminium signage, roll-up banner) turned out to be either a mockup carrying dlxprint's own "Deluxe Printing" watermark, or a real photo of one of dlxprint's actual clients' branding (Clean Co., Alvac Industry, Polygood/Danube, Pirham Health Care) — neither is something Creative Screens can show as its own work. None of dlxprint's product-category photos are safe to reuse without per-photo clearance from dlxprint, which we don't have. The earlier Unsplash photos for print-stationery/signage were also removed when this was tried, so all 26 non-gifts sub-categories are back to the concept plate until Creative Screens supplies its own project photos.

**Lesson for next time:** open and look at the actual photo (not just its filename/alt text) before reusing anything from another printing company's site — their mockups are frequently watermarked and their real-job photos frequently show the end client's logo.
